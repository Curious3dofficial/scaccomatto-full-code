# Scaccomatto 1.0.0

The first public release.

**Play**
- Eight variants: Classic, Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, Atomic, Fog of War and Duck Chess. Spell Chess is coming in a later update.
- Play against the bots Gary, Theo, Dex and Margot, each with their own style and story. Levi unlocks in a later update.
- Watch two bots play each other in Spectate.
- Play a friend anywhere: host a game and send them the six-letter code. On the same Wi-Fi it connects directly; otherwise it goes over the internet.
- Join Game finds you an opponent online who picked the same variant and clock.
- Play a friend on the same computer, too.

**Accounts (optional)**
- Sign in with your email or Google. You can still play everything as a guest.
- Pick a name and a picture that opponents see.
- Get a rating from Join Game matches against other signed-in players, and climb the leaderboard. Private games with a code are never rated.
- Your achievements and settings follow you to any computer you sign in on.
- Delete your account any time from Settings > Privacy.

**Learn**
- Analysis board with engine lines and an opening explorer.
- Game Review marks the best moves and the mistakes in any finished game.
- Set up any position and analyse it.

**Everything else**
- Achievements, three piece sets, board themes, and light and dark mode.
- Four menu themes that play in turn, or pick your favourite.
- Keyboard shortcuts throughout: arrow keys on the menus, Esc to go back, Ctrl + and Ctrl − to change the interface size, M to mute.
- No ads and no tracking. The privacy page in Settings > About says exactly what an account keeps.

**Install**
- Windows: run the `.exe`. If Windows warns you, click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- Linux: `sudo apt install ./scaccomatto_1.0.0_amd64.deb`
