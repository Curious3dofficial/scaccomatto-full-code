# Scaccomatto 1.1.0

**New in 1.1.0**
- Friends: add each other by username or from a player's card, see who's online, and challenge a friend straight into a game.
- Player cards: click your opponent's name for their picture, rating and your record against them.
- Mute, block and report players. Blocked players are never matched with you again.
- Both players' names and ratings now show above and below the board in online games.
- Signing up now confirms your email address first, and the account screens keep one size.
- Clearer messages when online play is busy or your internet is down.
- The download is about half the size it was: the music is now stored compressed.
- The end-of-game window now shows both players' pictures, bots included.
- Games against a bot, and bot-vs-bot matches, now wait for you to press Start Game, just like a game with a friend.
- Games and Analysis open faster, and the loading screen no longer freezes.
- The music now keeps playing during games and Analysis too.
- Settings > Audio has a new Output device option, so you can switch to headphones or earphones without restarting.
- Back buttons and sliding screens have their own sounds, and changing the board colour blends smoothly.
- Argus, the chess engine, now keeps itself up to date. New versions download quietly and take over the next time you open the game.


**Play**
- Eight variants: Classic, Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, Atomic, Fog of War and Duck Chess. Spell Chess is coming in a later update.
- Play against the bots Gary, Theo, Dex and Margot, each with their own style and story. Levi unlocks in a later update.
- Watch two bots play each other in Spectate.
- Play a friend anywhere: host a game and send them the six-letter code. On the same Wi-Fi it connects directly; otherwise it goes over the internet.
- Join Game finds you an opponent online who picked the same variant and clock.
- Play a friend on the same computer, too.

**Accounts (optional)**
- Sign in with your email or Google. You can still play everything as a guest.
- Pick a name and a picture that opponents see.
- Get a rating from Join Game matches against other signed-in players, and climb the leaderboard. Private games with a code are never rated.
- Your achievements and settings follow you to any computer you sign in on.
- Delete your account any time from Settings > Privacy.

**Learn**
- Analysis board with engine lines and an opening explorer.
- Game Review marks the best moves and the mistakes in any finished game.
- Set up any position and analyse it.

**Everything else**
- Achievements, three piece sets, board themes, and light and dark mode.
- Four menu themes that play in turn, or pick your favourite.
- Keyboard shortcuts throughout: arrow keys on the menus, Esc to go back, M to mute.
- No ads and no tracking. The privacy page in Settings > About says exactly what an account keeps.

**Install**
- Windows: run the `.exe`. If Windows warns you, click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- Linux: `sudo apt install ./scaccomatto_1.1.0_amd64.deb`
