# Scaccomatto 1.2.0

**New in 1.2.0**
- Country flags: pick your country in Settings > Online Play and your flag shows at the end of your name in online games. You can also choose not to show one.
- Titles: players rated 2200 and up get a CM, FM, IM or GM tag next to their name, in games, on player cards and in your friends list.
- New players now start at a rating of 200 instead of 1200.
- Focus mode: hides your opponent's name, rating, title, flag and picture so you can just play the board. Turn it on in Settings or from the in-game settings panel.
- Turn reminders: in online games longer than an hour, you get a notification when it's your move and the game isn't in front.
- Your friends and blocked players lists now show everyone's picture and whether they're online.
- Storage & Data: move where the game keeps its files, save your settings to a file and load them on another computer, see and remove saved games, or clear everything and start fresh.
- Seven new piece sets: Chessnut, Spatial, Papercut, Cburnett, Eyes, Skulls and Prmi (cats!).
- Help and About now open as a book you can flip through, and you can select and copy its text.
- Atomic: bigger explosions with their own sound, a cleaner move list, and a game that ends with the king blown up is marked with a *.
- When a king captures and the game ends, the result badge now appears on the square it captured on.
- The menu music now fades out smoothly when a game starts, in every mode.
- Switching between light and dark mode now crossfades instead of jumping.
- Open dropdown lists let you type to jump to an option (handy for the country list).
- The timeout badge now shows again when you step back to the last move.
- Fixed long profile names being cut off, and flags and pictures not showing for some online opponents.

**Play**
- Seven variants: Classic, Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, Atomic, Fog of War and Duck Chess. Spell Chess is coming in a later update.
- Play against the bots Gary, Theo, Dex and Margot, each with their own style and story. Levi unlocks in a later update.
- Watch two bots play each other in Spectate.
- Play a friend anywhere: host a game and send them the six-letter code. On the same Wi-Fi it connects directly; otherwise it goes over the internet.
- Join Game finds you an opponent online who picked the same variant and clock.
- Play a friend on the same computer, too.

**Accounts (optional)**
- Sign in with your email or Google. You can still play everything as a guest.
- Pick a name, a picture and a country that opponents see.
- Get a rating from Join Game matches against other signed-in players, and climb the leaderboard. Private games with a code are never rated.
- Add friends, see who's online, and challenge them straight into a game.
- Your achievements and settings follow you to any computer you sign in on.
- Delete your account any time from Settings > Privacy.

**Learn**
- Analysis board with engine lines and an opening explorer.
- Game Review marks the best moves and the mistakes in any finished game.
- Set up any position and analyse it.

**Everything else**
- Achievements, ten piece sets, board themes, and light and dark mode.
- Four menu themes that play in turn, or pick your favourite.
- Keyboard shortcuts throughout: arrow keys on the menus, Esc to go back, M to mute.
- No ads and no tracking. The privacy page in Settings > About says exactly what an account keeps.

**Install**
- Windows: run the `.exe`. If Windows warns you, click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- Linux: `sudo apt install ./scaccomatto_1.2.0_amd64.deb`
