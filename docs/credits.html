Scaccomatto

Credits

Scaccomatto is designed and built by Alekhine Studios. The chess engine, Argus, was written from scratch for it: it plays every bot, runs the analysis and powers Game Review. The bots, their stories and artwork, and Spell Chess were made for Scaccomatto too.

Everything below is other people's work, used under the licence it was shared with. Thank you.
