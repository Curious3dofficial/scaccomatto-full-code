Scaccomatto

Credits

Scaccomatto is designed and built by Alekhine Studios Private Limited. The chess engine, Argus, was written from scratch for it: it plays every bot, runs the analysis and powers Game Review. The bots, their stories and artwork, and Spell Chess were made for Scaccomatto too.
