Eight ways to play

Classic, Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, Atomic, Fog of War and Duck Chess, with Spell Chess on the way. Play them against a friend on the same computer, or against anyone online.

Bots with a backstory

Every bot has a name, a story and a way of playing. Gary will grab anything you leave lying around; the others are harder to fool. Each one has its own strengths and blind spots, so beating one feels different from beating the next.

See where it went wrong

Game Review walks back through your game and marks the good moves and the blunders. The engine behind it, Argus, was written from scratch for Scaccomatto.

Play anyone, anywhere

Host a game and send your friend the six-letter code, wherever they are, or press Join Game to be matched with someone who wants the same variant and clock. Sign in to add friends, see who's online and challenge them, and to climb the leaderboard. Click any opponent's name for their player card.

Installing

Windows

  1. Run Scaccomatto-Setup and follow the steps.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the installer is new and isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  3. Open Scaccomatto from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

Updating? Remove the old version first if the installer asks you to Change, Repair or Remove.

Linux

  1. Download the .deb file.
  2. In a terminal, in the folder you saved it to:
    sudo apt install ./scaccomatto_*_amd64.deb
  3. Open Scaccomatto from your app menu.

To remove it later: sudo apt remove scaccomatto

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads and nothing to buy. An account is optional.

What about Mac?

Not yet. Windows and Linux come first.

Can I play people over the internet?

Yes. Send a friend your game's six-letter code, or use Join Game to be matched with someone. Games between two signed-in players from Join Game are rated.

Do I need an account? What does it keep?

No, you can play everything as a guest. If you make one, it keeps your email, name, picture, rating, settings and achievements, and you can delete it from Settings any time. The privacy page has the details.

Something broke. Where do I report it?

Email alekhinestudios@gmail.com. If the app saved a problem report (Settings → About → Problem reports), attaching it helps a lot.
